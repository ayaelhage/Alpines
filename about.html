01 / Our story

A different way
to see winter.

Most travel sites treat winter as the quiet season. We think it's the best one.

Alpines began with a simple question: where does winter actually change a place? Roads disappear under snow, mountains become ski routes, and small towns glow through short days. We picked five destinations where that change is the whole point, and built the site around them.

02 / How it works

From idea to departure in four steps.

  1. 01

    Pick a destination

    Start on the map. Choose one of the five countries and the boarding pass shows what winter looks like there.

  2. 02

    Choose a package

    Each package is built around one experience: northern lights, glaciers, onsens, fjords or skiing.

  3. 03

    Check the details

    Nights, what's included and the starting price are shown up front, so nothing is hidden in small print.

  4. 04

    Go in winter

    Every trip is timed for the best weeks of the season, because the right month changes the whole trip.

03 / Behind Alpines

Built by one person, one feature at a time.

Hi, I'm Aya Hage. I created Alpines as a personal web development project, combining my interest in travel with the challenge of building an interactive website from the ground up.

Everything here is written by hand in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript: the interactive map, the boarding pass and the endless carousel. It is now growing into a full-stack travel application, with a backend and database next.

04 / Questions

Good to know before you go.

Short answers to the things people ask most. Something missing? Send a message below.

Can I book a trip right now?

Not yet. Alpines is a portfolio project, so the booking flow, accounts and payments are still on the roadmap. Right now you can explore every destination and package.

Why only winter?

Because winter changes places more than any other season. We would rather show five destinations at their best than fifty at their average.

What does "food included" mean?

Each package card says whether meals are part of the price. If it says "Food not included", you plan your own meals.

What should I pack?

Warm layers, waterproof boots with good grip, gloves, a hat and sunglasses (snow is bright). Every package page will list anything specific to that trip.

Which destination should I start with?

Chasing the northern lights points to Finland, Iceland or Norway. For hot springs, Japan. For skiing, Switzerland. The packages carousel highlights what each trip includes.
