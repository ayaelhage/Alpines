why us?

We travel differently when the world turns cold.

Alpines began with a simple idea: winter should not be treated as the season between trips.

Some places become completely different once the temperature drops. Roads disappear beneath snow, mountains turn into ski routes, quiet towns glow beneath shorter days, and landscapes that already felt remote become something else entirely.

We focus on five destinations that each offer their own version of winter — from the northern lights of Finland and Iceland, to alpine routes in Switzerland, fjords in Norway and snowy towns and onsens in Japan.

01

Winter-led routes

We choose destinations around what makes them worth experiencing during winter, instead of taking a regular itinerary and simply adding snow.

02

Experience over checklists

The journey is built around the reason you wanted to go in the first place — auroras, skiing, frozen landscapes, hot springs, quiet roads and the feeling of being somewhere shaped by the season.

03

Five very different winters

Finland, Iceland, Japan, Norway and Switzerland are not interchangeable. Each destination offers a different landscape, pace and way of experiencing the cold.
